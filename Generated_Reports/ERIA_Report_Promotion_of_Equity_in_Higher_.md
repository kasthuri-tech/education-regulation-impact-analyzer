# Regulatory Impact Report: UGC Promotion of Equity in Higher Education Institutions Regulations, 2026

## 📊 Document Executive Summary
* **Category:** Institutional Governance
* **Compliance Tone:** High Compliance Workload
* **Length/Density:** 95 characters

### Layman Summary
* Mandates the strict prevention of discrimination against SC, ST, OBC, Minorities, Women, and Differently-abled students in all HEIs.
* Requires every university and college to establish a dedicated 'Equal Opportunity Cell' (EOC) within 60 days.
* Mandates the appointment of a senior professor as the 'Anti-Discrimination Officer' (ADO) to head local grievance boards.
* Enforces strict timelines: all discrimination complaints must be investigated and resolved within fifteen (15) working days.
* Prohibits any form of harassment, hate speech, or segregated seating/dining in academic and residential blocks.
* Requires HEIs to publish a transparent student charter outlining safety pathways and anti-discrimination rights.
* Failing to implement these measures will result in severe penalties, including stripping of university status and suspension of research development funds.

* **Benefits:** Strict, standardized equity audits across all universities.
