# 🌐 Official Education Policy & Regulation Reference Links

This document lists the official portals and reference links used for obtaining education regulations, university circulars, and accreditation guidelines for testing the **ERIA (Education Regulation Impact Analyzer)**.

You can paste any of these URLs directly into the **ERIA Scraper Panel** in your Streamlit dashboard!

---

## 🏛️ 1. University Grants Commission (UGC) Portals

The UGC is the primary regulatory body for higher education in India. You can fetch active notices, regulations, and guidelines from:

*   **📢 Official UGC Notices:** [https://www.ugc.gov.in/Notices](https://www.ugc.gov.in/Notices?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    *   *Usage:* General updates, administrative notifications, and urgent compliance requests for HEIs.
*   **📜 Official UGC Regulations:** [https://www.ugc.gov.in/regulations](https://www.ugc.gov.in/regulations?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    *   *Usage:* Heavy legislative documents outlining minimum standards for teaching, research, and degree awards.
*   **📝 Official UGC Circulars:** [https://www.ugc.gov.in/Circulars](https://www.ugc.gov.in/Circulars?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    *   *Usage:* Official directives to Vice Chancellors and college principals regarding national programs and student safety.
*   **📖 Official UGC Guidelines:** [https://www.ugc.gov.in/Guideline](https://www.ugc.gov.in/Guideline?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    *   *Usage:* Step-by-step guidance on curricular credit designs, student internship patterns, and skill-based learnings.

## 🏛️ 2. Optional Academic & Accreditation References

These portals can also serve as excellent test sources for evaluating ERIA's general NLP classification capability:

*   **🛠️ AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education):** [https://www.aicte-india.org/](https://www.aicte-india.org/)
    *   *Focus:* Technical, management, and engineering circulars.
*   **🎓 NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council):** [http://www.naac.gov.in/](http://www.naac.gov.in/)
    *   *Focus:* Institutional quality audits, grading benchmarks, and self-study report structures.
*   **📊 NIRF (National Institutional Ranking Framework):** [https://www.nirfindia.org/](https://www.nirfindia.org/)
    *   *Focus:* Ranking guidelines, research parameters, and graduate outcome indicators.
*   **🏫 Ministry of Education, Government of India:** [https://www.education.gov.in/](https://www.education.gov.in/)
    *   *Focus:* Macro educational guidelines (e.g. National Education Policy - NEP).

---

## 🚀 How to Scrape and Analyze an Online Notice in ERIA

1. Open the **ERIA Streamlit Dashboard** in your browser (`http://localhost:8501`).
2. In the left-hand sidebar, select **"Scrape UGC / Academic URL"** as your Input Source.
3. Paste any URL from the portals above (for example, a link to a specific notice webpage on `ugc.gov.in`).
4. Click **"Fetch URL Content"**.
5. Once the raw text is successfully scraped and previewed, click the primary blue **🚀 Analyze Regulation** button!
