NEW DELHI

Date: 5th May, 2026

REGULATORY NOTIFICATION

Subject: UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Second Amendment Regulations, 2026.

1. Objective:
To maintain standards and quality in non-traditional learning modes, the UGC hereby notifies the strict conditions under which colleges and universities may offer degrees through Open and Distance Learning (ODL) and Online modes.

2. Mandatory Eligibility for Institutions:
Only Higher Educational Institutions (HEIs) meeting the following criteria are permitted to offer ODL and/or Online degrees:
(a) NAAC Score: The institution must possess a minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 3.26 on a 4-point scale from the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC).
(b) NIRF Ranking: The university must rank in the Top-100 in the University category of the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) at least twice in the three preceding ranking cycles.
(c) Prior Approval: No ODL or Online program shall be launched without formal prior approval from the Distance Education Bureau (DEB) of the UGC.

3. Admissions and Examinations Compliance:
(a) Standardized Admissions: Online program admissions must be carried out through a transparent, centralized online portal with mandatory biometric identification logs.
(b) Proctored Examinations: All examinations for Online and ODL courses must be conducted through computer-based tests (CBT) under web-proctored surveillance, or physically in designated, approved regional centers.
(c) Credit Equivalency: Degrees awarded through ODL and Online modes from UGC-recognized HEIs shall be treated as equivalent to degrees awarded through conventional offline modes for employment and higher studies.

4. Penal Provisions for Violators:
Any HEI offering ODL or Online programs without DEB approval or failing to meet the NAAC score of 3.26 shall face:
- Immediate cancellation of approval for all courses.
- A penalty fine of up to INR 10 Lakhs.
- Categorization of the institution as a "Defaulting ODL/Online Institution" in public notices.
