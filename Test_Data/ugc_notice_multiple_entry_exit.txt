UNIVERSITY GRANTS COMMISSION (UGC)
NOTIFICATION
NEW DELHI

No. F. 1-2/2026(UGC/NEP_MEE)
Date: 22nd February, 2026

Subject: Guidelines for Multiple Entry and Exit in Academic Programmes Offered in Higher Education Institutions (HEIs).

1. INTRODUCTION & VISION:
In alignment with the National Education Policy (NEP), the University Grants Commission hereby issues guidelines to enable modularity and structural flexibility in higher education. The traditional system of higher education imposes rigid boundaries, often penalizing students who are forced to drop out due to financial, health, or personal constraints by providing them no formal certification. The Multiple Entry and Exit system seeks to remove these boundaries.

2. STRUCTURE OF MULTIPLE ENTRY AND EXIT:
The higher education framework shall be structured modularly as follows:
(a) First Year: A student exiting at the end of the first year (two semesters), having successfully completed the required 40 credits and a mandatory 4-week vocational internship, shall be awarded an Undergraduate Certificate in the respective discipline.
(b) Second Year: A student exiting at the end of the second year (four semesters), completing 80 credits, shall be awarded an Undergraduate Diploma.
(c) Third Year: A student exiting at the end of the third year (six semesters), completing 120 credits, shall be awarded a Bachelor's Degree.
(d) Fourth Year: A student completing a four-year program (eight semesters) with 160 credits, including a mandatory research dissertation, shall be awarded a Bachelor's Degree (Honours with Research).

3. GUIDELINES FOR RE-ENTRY:
(a) Validity of Credits: The academic credits earned by a student shall be stored in their Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) portal. Credits remain valid for a maximum of seven (7) academic years.
(b) Re-entry Window: A student who exited with a Certificate or Diploma can apply for re-entry to the next level of the program at any registered HEI within the 7-year credit validity period.
(c) Lateral Entry Intake: HEIs shall set aside 10% supernumerary seats in the second and third years of undergraduate courses to accommodate lateral-entry students returning to complete their degrees.

4. ADMINISTRATIVE WORKLOAD & REGISTRATION:
To facilitate this system, all colleges and universities must:
- Implement a credit-based semester system aligned with the National Credit Framework (NCrF).
- Redesign syllabus modules to ensure each year represents a self-contained unit of learning.
- Configure their campus databases to upload transcripts to the DigiLocker-based ABC portal continuously.

Failure of universities to implement multiple entry-exit options for students starting in the academic session 2026-27 shall attract warnings during NAAC assessments and public notices on the UGC portal.
